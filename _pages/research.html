Research

My research measures how climate change erodes tropical biodiversity, and turns that evidence into protection decisions. In my PhD I modelled three decades of Wet Tropics monitoring to explain rainforest bird and possum declines. As a postdoctoral fellow I forecast heatwave risk for the endangered spectacled flying fox, so managers can act before the next mass mortality event.
