Alejandro de la Fuente, PhD

AI engineer, raised on rainforest data and honest error bars.

Hi, I'm Alejandro. My grandfather walked me into the forests of rural Spain as a child, and I never really left the natural world. A decade of quantitative ecology on three continents followed, from pumas in the Andes to possums in Australian rainforest.

I built Bayesian forecasts of wildlife populations, where a wrong number could cost a species its protection. That discipline now drives my work as an AI engineer: explicit assumptions, calibrated uncertainty, and evaluation that finds failure before users do. I like hard problems and tools people actually use.
