Styleguide

Internal reference for the design system: every token, every type step, the fourteen components from the brief plus the Phase 4 additions, in both themes. Use the toggle in the header to switch theme.

Foundations

Colour tokens

Nine tokens, defined once in _sass/_tokens.scss as CSS custom properties. No hex value exists outside that file. Swatches render the live token for the active theme; the two values under each name are light and dark.

  • --paper#FAFAF7 / #1F1E1D
  • --paper-sunk#F0EEE6 / #2B2A28
  • --ink#141413 / #F2F0E9
  • --ink-muted#6B6960 / #A3A199
  • --ink-faint#93918A / #6E6C65
  • --rule#E5E3DB / #3A3936
  • --accent#D97757 / #E08A6C
  • --accent-deep#C15F3C / #F0A98D
  • --accent-wash#F7EDE7 / #2E2622

Accent rules: links and active nav carry the accent as an underline, never as body-size text colour. At most one emphasis element per screen. Never a large fill.

Foundations

Typography

Three families, self-hosted as variable woff2 with font-display: swap, latin subset with Spanish diacritics. Newsreader for display, Inter for body and UI, JetBrains Mono for code and data columns.

A well-set print journal that happens to be a website. Años entre montañas: ¿qué pájaro canta? ¡El chucao!

Inter

Body and interface text. Warm paper, near-black ink, hairline rules. Señales añejas: ¿cuántas quedan? ¡Muchísimas!

Motion

150ms to 200ms, ease-out, colour and opacity only. No entrance animations, no scroll reveals, no parallax, no counters. Everything is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. Hover the sample:

Colour and opacity only

Foundations

Dark mode

The theme follows prefers-color-scheme until the header toggle sets an explicit choice, stored in localStorage. An inline blocking script in the head applies the stored choice before first paint, so a hard reload never flashes the wrong theme.

Component 9 of 14

Disclosure block

An appendix, not hidden content: the original abstract survives verbatim beneath the plain summary on every paper page. Native details, keyboard accessible, printable heading.

Original abstract as published

Aim. The increasing frequency and intensity of extreme weather escalate the pressure of global warming on biodiversity. Globally, synergistic effects of multiple components of climate change have driven local extinctions and community collapses, raising concern about the irreversible deterioration of ecosystems. Here, we disentangle the pressure of increasing warming and frequency of extreme heatwaves on the population dynamics of tropical ringtail possums (family: Pseudocheiridae).

Location. The Australian Wet Tropics World Heritage Area.

Method. Ringtail possums' population dynamics were estimated between 1992 and 2021 using a hierarchical population model that explicitly described the state process and accounted for imperfect detection. Under our model, we propagated the estimated mechanisms governing the system by forecasting ringtails' population dynamics between 2022 and 2050. Derived from this process, we calculated the probability of absolute and quasi-extinction using different population viability thresholds.

Results. We find a strong negative effect of climate change on population dynamics, particularly extreme heatwaves, resulting in a rapid and severe decline in ringtails' population size in the last three decades.

Main conclusions. Forecasted increases in temperature and heatwaves threaten the collapse of rain forest ringtail possums by 2050, with populations falling below viability thresholds within three decades.
