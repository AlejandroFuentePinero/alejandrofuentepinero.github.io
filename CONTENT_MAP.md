### 2.1 Mechanism notes

- `jekyll-redirect-from` is already configured and Pages-whitelisted. Plain targets get a `redirect_from` entry on the destination page. Targets with an anchor (for example `/research/#talks`) need a small stub file with `redirect_to`, because `redirect_from` cannot carry a fragment. Phase 2 creates the stubs and lists every line in MIGRATIONS.md.
- Publication and talk permalinks have no trailing slash, so Jekyll serves them as `.html` files. MIGRATIONS.md must cover both the extensionless and the `.html` form of each; the table below lists the canonical form once.
- The two publication URLs containing spaces and percent-encoding get their exact old strings as redirects, in both encoded and literal forms (section 2.5). Phase 2 tests these locally before anything moves (AUDIT.md 9.4a).
- Pages whose content splits across two destinations get one primary redirect target, chosen below (AUDIT.md 9.4b).

## 7. The worked publication summary

The standard, demonstrated once in full before Phase 5 applies it 12 times. Chosen paper: **Climate change threatens the future of rain forest ringtail possums by 2050** (Diversity and Distributions, 2022), because it is the thesis in miniature: a Bayesian forecast with a legal consequence.

### 7.1 Index entry (25-word ceiling)

> Ringtail possum populations in the Wet Tropics fall below viability thresholds by 2050 under forecast warming. Extreme heatwaves do most of the damage.

23 words. A finding, not a topic, per brief 4.5.

### 7.2 The plain-language summary (120-word ceiling)

Written fresh from the finding, not paraphrased from the abstract. Tier B: STE rules, natural voice.

> Ringtail possums in the Australian Wet Tropics are collapsing, and extreme heatwaves are the main driver. We built a hierarchical population model on 30 years of survey data, from 1992 to 2021. The model separates real population change from imperfect detection, so a trend is never an artefact of who searched where. It shows that warming, and heatwaves in particular, drove a rapid and severe decline over three decades.
>
> We then ran the fitted model forward. Under forecast warming, populations fall below viability thresholds by 2050. We used the result to nominate the lemuroid ringtail possum for listing under the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act. The story reached 70 national news outlets.

### 7.4 Page structure around it

Top to bottom: the summary above; "Original abstract as published" as a collapsed block, verbatim, untouched; the citation string, untouched; the DOI link; the media line (70 national stories) with its Mediaportal source; a cross-link to the forecasting project page.

### 7.5 Audience check

The recruiter gets a sentence they can repeat in a phone screen: he forecast a species collapse and the forecast fed a national protection decision. The technical peer gets the method skeleton (observation separated from state process, forecasts propagated from a fitted mechanism) and a path to level 3. The academic gets the finding stated accurately, with the real abstract one click below and nothing rewritten over their field's conventions.
